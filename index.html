01 / Our purpose

We believe the future of learning should be more personal, more accessible, and more human—not simply more automated.

02 / Research

Questions worth exploring.

Our work connects learning science, responsible AI, and real classroom needs.

Personalized learning

How can AI adapt explanations, practice, and feedback to different learners without narrowing their curiosity or agency?

Teacher augmentation

How can intelligent tools reduce repetitive work and surface useful insights while preserving teachers’ expertise and control?

Responsible learning systems

How do we evaluate educational AI for accuracy, fairness, transparency, privacy, and meaningful learning outcomes?

Learning analytics

How can evidence about the learning process become understandable and actionable for students and educators?
